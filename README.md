## 💙 About Calmi

Calmi is an AI-powered mental wellness companion designed to offer a welcoming space where users can express their thoughts and feelings.

By combining generative AI, user authentication, and persistent chat storage, Calmi provides a simple web experience for supportive conversations and everyday emotional reflection.

> *Talk freely. Take a breath. Find your calm.*

## ✨ Features

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%">
      <h3>🤍 AI-Powered Conversations</h3>
      Supportive, empathetic responses generated using Google Gemini.
    </td>
    <td width="50%">
      <h3>🔐 Secure Authentication</h3>
      User registration, password hashing, JWT authentication, and HTTP-only cookies.
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%">
      <h3>💬 Persistent Chat History</h3>
      Store conversations in MongoDB and retrieve recent messages for AI context.
    </td>
    <td width="50%">
      <h3>🔄 AI Fallback Support</h3>
      Groq integration as a fallback provider for AI-generated responses.
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%">
      <h3>🫶 Crisis-Aware Responses</h3>
      Predefined supportive responses for certain crisis-related keywords.
    </td>
    <td width="50%">
      <h3>🧹 Chat Management</h3>
      Clear saved conversation history whenever needed.
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

Calmi is a mental wellness companion, not a replacement for professional mental health care, diagnosis, medical advice, or emergency services. AI-generated responses may be inaccurate or incomplete, and keyword-based crisis detection cannot identify every emergency.

If you are in immediate danger or experiencing a crisis, contact local emergency services or a qualified professional.
